= Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Spring Data DynamoDB is expected to help create a
respectful, welcoming community, regardless of experience, background, identity,
disability, or personal beliefs.

== Expected behavior

* Treat others with courtesy and respect.
* Discuss ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
* Respect privacy, differing experiences, and reasonable boundaries.
* Accept corrections and take responsibility for the effect of your behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, personal
attacks, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

== Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and community
spaces, and to people representing the project in public.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or remove a participant from the community. Decisions should be
proportionate, explained to the people involved when appropriate, and respectful
of reporter privacy. Retaliation against a good-faith report is unacceptable.

== Reporting

Contact the maintainers through a private contact channel listed on the
https://github.com/Not-Your-Average-Dev[Not Your Average Dev organization profile].
Include the relevant links and a short account of what happened. Do not publish
private information in a public issue. GitHub's report-abuse tools are also
available for conduct occurring on GitHub.

Before opening the project to public contributions, maintainers must publish and
verify a private conduct-reporting contact on the organization profile. Reports
will be handled discreetly; information will be shared only as needed to review
and respond to the incident.
